▧素材与权限Media & permissions

提示没有相册权限，怎么办？Why does the app ask for Photos access?

前往 iPhone 或 iPad 的“设置”找到“帧间”，检查相册访问权限。选择需要使用的照片时，也可以在系统选择器中重新授权。授权后返回 App 重试。Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, find Frame and check its Photos access. You can also reselect media using the system picker. Return to the app and try again.

为什么会提示“第 X 张素材加载失败”？What does “Media item X failed to load” mean?

先确认该素材在系统“照片”中可以正常打开；如果它存储在 iCloud，请等待原始文件下载完成。然后重新选择素材并重试；若仅某一张失败，可换一张照片定位问题。Check that the item opens in Photos. If it is stored in iCloud, wait for the original to download. Then select it again. If only one item fails, try another to narrow down the cause.

▦创作与导出Creation & export

Live Photo 串接为什么需要至少两张素材？Why does Live Photo stitching require two items?

串接功能需要至少两张真正的 Live Photo。请在系统“照片”中确认每张素材都能长按播放，并重新选择；普通静态照片不能作为串接的动态素材。Stitching requires at least two actual Live Photos. Check that each item plays when pressed and held in Photos, then select them again. Still images do not provide motion for stitching.

导出 Live Photo 时显示“导出已中止”？Why was my Live Photo export interrupted?

处理 Live Photo 视频时请保持 App 在前台，不要锁屏或切换至其他 App，直到保存完成。也请检查设备剩余空间和相册访问权限，然后再次导出。Keep Frame in the foreground while the Live Photo video is processed. Avoid locking the screen or switching apps until it saves. Check free storage and Photos access before retrying.

如何把照片拆成四图或九宫格？How do I split a photo into a 2×2 or 3×3 grid?

进入“拆图”，选择照片后设置“四图（2 × 2）”或“九图（3 × 3）”。点击预览可调整裁切、缩放与旋转，确认后导出到相册。Open Grid Split, select a photo and choose 2×2 or 3×3. Tap the preview to adjust crop, zoom and rotation, then export to Photos.

✧广告与购买Ads & purchases

广告解锁提示“无广告可展示”，怎么办？What if no rewarded ad is available?

广告是否可用会随网络、地区和广告供应情况变化。请检查网络连接，稍后重试；“无广告可展示”不一定代表相册或网络权限被拒绝。如果需要立即使用高级功能，也可以选择升级 Pro。Ad availability varies by network, region and inventory. Check your connection and try again later. “No ad to show” does not necessarily mean your permissions were denied. Pro is another option if you need advanced features now.

提示“网络未连接”或广告网络连接异常？What if the ad network cannot connect?

检查当前网络；如果设备限制了 App 联网，在“设置 → 帧间 → 无线数据”中允许 WLAN 与蜂窝网络后重试。不同设备和系统版本中的设置名称可能略有差异。Check your connection and your device’s network permissions for Frame in Settings. On some devices you can choose Wireless Data → WLAN & Cellular Data. Setting names may differ across devices and iOS versions.

购买 Pro 后如何恢复？How do I restore Pro?

在 App 的高级功能区域点击“恢复购买”，并确认设备登录了当初购买时使用的 Apple 账户。如果仍无法恢复，请记录错误提示并检查 App Store 连接。Tap Restore Purchases in the app’s advanced features panel. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. If it still fails, note the error and check your App Store connection.

小提示：Tip: 观看一次奖励广告可获得 1 次高级功能使用机会；转换、串接与完整 Live Photo 导出共享次数。永久解锁后无需再观看广告。A completed rewarded ad grants one use of an advanced feature. Conversion, stitching and full Live Photo export share these credits. A permanent unlock removes the ad requirement.
