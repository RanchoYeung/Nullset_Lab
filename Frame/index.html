CREATIVE TOOLKIT / 01

灵感来了，工具刚好在。A tool for every idea.

从一张照片到一段动态影像，把创作的每一步留在同一个地方。From a single photo to a moving moment, everything you need is right here.

▦

自由拼接Creative collages

横排、纵排、田字格、九宫格，还有大图聚焦与杂志拼贴。选择布局，组合你的画面。Choose from rows, columns, 2×2, 3×3, featured-image and magazine-inspired layouts to bring your photos together.

⊞

四图 / 九宫格拆图Grid splitter

将一张照片拆为 2×2 或 3×3，调整裁切、缩放和旋转，再保存到相册。Split one photo into a 2×2 or 3×3 grid. Fine-tune crop, zoom and rotation before saving.

↝

视频 / GIF 转 Live PhotoVideo / GIF to Live Photo

选取视频或 GIF，挑好封面与片段，把喜欢的瞬间变成可长按播放的 Live Photo。Pick a video or GIF, choose its cover and moment, and save it as a press-to-play Live Photo.

▤

Live Photo 串接Live Photo stitching

让两张或更多 Live Photo 连成一段，记录不必在一次快门后结束。Join two or more Live Photos into one continuous moment. Let your story keep moving.

✧

批量水印Batch watermarks

使用文字或 PNG / GIF 素材，为照片统一加上你的署名与风格。Add text or PNG / GIF watermarks to your photos for a consistent signature and style.

SIMPLE BY DESIGN / 02

三步，把灵感变成作品。From idea to finished frame.

01

选择玩法Pick your canvas

拼接、拆图、转换、串接或水印，按想法进入对应工具。Choose a collage, grid, conversion, stitch or watermark tool to match your idea.

02

加入素材Add your media

从相册选择照片、Live Photo、视频或 GIF，并预览与调整。Select photos, Live Photos, videos or GIFs from your library, then preview and refine.

03

保存作品Save your creation

确认效果后导出到相册，让创作成为真正可以分享的画面。Export the finished result to Photos, ready to keep or share.
