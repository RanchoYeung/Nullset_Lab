This application is developed by Nullset Lab. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use this application. 1. Information We Collect By default, this application does not collect, store, or upload any personally identifiable information. Specifically: * No account registration is required. * We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information. * We do not create user profiles. * We do not sell or rent your personal information. * We do not use your information for advertising or marketing purposes. 2. Location Permission If you choose to enable the Weather feature, the application may request access to your device’s location. Your location is used solely to retrieve weather information for your current location. It is not stored by the developer and is not uploaded to the developer’s servers. You may revoke this permission at any time through your device settings. Disabling location access will only affect the Weather feature and will not impact the core clock or Pomodoro Timer functions. 3. Internet Connection Most features of this application work completely offline. An internet connection is required only when retrieving weather information. 4. Local Data Storage The following information is stored only on your device: * Clock display settings * Appearance and theme preferences * Pomodoro Timer configurations * Weather feature preferences * Other application settings This information is never uploaded to any developer-operated server. 5. Third-Party Services The Weather feature may use third-party weather service providers to obtain weather information. Only the minimum information necessary to provide weather services (such as your device location) may be shared with these providers. Their handling of your information is governed by their own privacy policies. Other than weather services, this application does not intentionally share your personal information with any third party. 6. Children’s Privacy This application is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through this application, please contact us and we will take appropriate action where applicable. 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the application evolves. Any updates will be published on this page and become effective immediately upon publication. 8.
