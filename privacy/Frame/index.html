隐私政策

最后更新：2026-10-27

欢迎使用帧间（Frame）。

本应用由空集实验室（Nullset Lab）开发。我们尊重并重视您的隐私。本隐私政策说明了本应用如何收集、使用和保护您的信息。

1. 信息收集

本应用默认不会收集、存储或上传您的个人身份信息。

  • 无需注册账号；
  • 不收集您的姓名、邮箱、电话号码等个人信息；
  • 不建立用户档案；
  • 不出售或出租您的个人信息；
  • 由开发者运营的服务器不存在（本应用没有自有服务器），不会上传您选择的照片或 Live Photo。

需要说明的是：本应用包含第三方广告服务（Google AdMob），仅在您主动点击“观看激励广告以解锁高级功能”时才会加载并展示广告。广告服务提供商可能收集的信息（如设备标识、IP 地址等）请见第 5 节。

2. 照片与相册权限

本应用的核心功能（拼接、拆图、转换、串接、水印）需要访问您的照片：

  • 选择素材时：本应用使用 iOS 系统照片选择器（Photos Picker）。该选择器由 iOS 在系统进程中运行，只有您明确选择的素材会被提供给本应用。您可以在 iOS 设置中将本应用的照片访问范围限定为“所选照片”。
  • 导出时：保存导出结果需要“添加到照片”权限。本应用只会写入您主动导出的作品，不会读取或修改您已有的其他照片。
  • iCloud 照片：如果您选择的素材原片仅存于 iCloud 且尚未下载到设备，系统会自动下载该原片以供处理。此行为仅发生在您选择该素材时，由 Apple 的照片框架完成。

您可以随时在 iOS 系统设置中关闭或调整相册权限。关闭后，需要素材的功能（拼接、拆图、转换等）将无法选择照片，但不影响 App 的其他设置。

本应用不会请求定位、麦克风、通讯录等其他权限，也不会访问您未明确选择的任何照片。

3. 网络连接

本应用的所有核心功能（拼接、拆图、水印渲染等）均可在本地完成，本应用不搭建、不维护任何自有服务器，不会将您选择的照片或 Live Photo 上传至任何服务器。

仅在以下场景需要连接互联网：

  • 您主动点击“观看激励广告解锁”后，加载与展示激励广告；
  • App Store 内购（购买、恢复购买）与 Apple 的通信；
  • 您选择的照片原片存于 iCloud 时，由 Apple 照片框架下载原片。

若您的素材全部存于本机，则除广告解锁外通常无需联网。

4. 本地数据存储

本应用会将以下数据仅存储在您的设备本地（系统偏好存储中）：

  • 高级功能的解锁状态与激励广告次数额度；
  • 导出画质等用户偏好设置；
  • 其他用户偏好（如水印配置）。

上述数据不会同步至任何开发者服务器，卸载 App 即被删除。

5. 第三方服务

本应用使用以下第三方服务：

  • Google AdMob（激励广告）：用于解锁高级功能。仅当您主动点击解锁时，广告才会加载并展示；广告 SDK 只在此之后才进行初始化。Google 可能收集设备信息、广告标识、IP 地址等用于广告投放与反作弊，其数据处理受 Google 隐私政策 约束。您可在广告展示前的隐私选项（Google 提供的同意界面）中管理相关同意。
  • Apple App Store（内购）：完整版功能通过 App Store 内购解锁。购买与恢复购买由 Apple 处理，开发者不会收到您的支付账户信息。受 Apple 隐私政策 约束。
  • Apple iCloud 照片：处理 iCloud 中的素材时按第 2 节所述行为，由 Apple 的框架完成。

除上述服务外，本应用不会主动与其他第三方共享您的任何信息。

6. 儿童隐私

本应用不会有意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息。

如果您认为儿童向本应用提供了相关信息，请联系我们，我们将在合理时间内删除相关信息（如适用）。

7. 隐私政策更新

随着应用功能的更新，本隐私政策可能会进行修改。

更新后的版本将在本页面发布，并自发布之日起生效。

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-10-27

Welcome to Frame.

This application is developed by Nullset Lab. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use this application.

1. Information We Collect

By default, this application does not collect, store, or upload any personally identifiable information.

  • No account registration is required.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information.
  • We do not create user profiles.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • The developer operates no servers of its own; this application does not upload the photos or Live Photos you select to any server.

Please note: this application contains third-party advertising (Google AdMob), used to unlock advanced features. Ads are only loaded and displayed when you actively choose to watch a rewarded ad. For information collected by the ad provider, see Section 5.

2. Photo & Library Access

The core features of this application (collage, grid split, conversion, stitching, watermarking) work with your photos:

  • Selecting assets: This app uses the iOS system Photos Picker, which runs in an iOS system process and only provides the assets you explicitly select. You may also limit this app's photo access to “Selected Photos” in iOS Settings.
  • Exporting: Saving an export requires the “Add to Photos” permission. The app only writes the works you explicitly export, and never reads or modifies your other photos.
  • iCloud Photos: If a selected asset exists only in iCloud, the system automatically downloads the original for processing. This happens only when you select that asset and is performed by Apple's photo framework.

You may revoke or adjust library access at any time in iOS Settings. Features requiring assets will stop working, but other app settings remain unaffected.

This app requests no location, microphone, contacts, or other permissions, and never accesses photos you have not explicitly selected.

3. Internet Connection

All core features (collage, grid split, watermark rendering, etc.) are performed on-device. The developer operates no servers, and your selected photos or Live Photos are never uploaded to any server.

An internet connection is required only in the following cases:

  • After you actively tap “watch an ad to unlock”, for loading and displaying the rewarded ad;
  • For App Store in-app purchases (purchasing, restoring) with Apple;
  • When a selected photo's original exists only in iCloud, downloaded by Apple's photo framework.

If all your assets are stored locally, no connection is typically needed except for ad unlocks.

4. Local Data Storage

The following information is stored only on your device (system preferences storage):

  • Unlock status of advanced features and rewarded-ad credit counts;
  • User preferences such as export quality;
  • Other user preferences (e.g., watermark configurations).

This information is never synced to any developer-operated server and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

5. Third-Party Services

This application uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (rewarded ads): Used to unlock advanced features. Ads are loaded and displayed only after you actively choose to, and the ad SDK initializes only at that point. Google may collect device information, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, etc., for ad delivery and fraud prevention, governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage consent through the privacy options (Google's consent form) shown before ads.
  • Apple App Store (In-App Purchases): Full access is unlocked via App Store purchase. Purchases and restorations are processed by Apple; the developer never receives your payment account details. Governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Apple iCloud Photos: As described in Section 2, performed by Apple's framework when processing iCloud-held assets.

Other than the above, this application does not intentionally share your information with any third party.

6. Children's Privacy

This application is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through this application, please contact us and we will take appropriate action where applicable.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the application evolves.

Any updates will be published on this page and become effective immediately upon publication.
